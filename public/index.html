HELP CENTRE & FAQ
How does End-to-End Encryption work?
Your personal messages, calls, photos, and voice notes are secured end-to-end. Only you and the person you're communicating with can read or listen to them. QuickChat servers never store unencrypted message payloads.
Who is ERA and how to use it?
ERA is your built-in intelligent AI assistant created by Anshuu (Anshul Kushwaha). You can chat with ERA directly in your contact list for ultra-fast answers, image generation, math, and smart replies in English and Hindi.
How do Voice and Video calls work?
QuickChat uses ultra-low latency WebRTC peer-to-peer audio and video streams with HD noise cancellation and DTLS encryption.
How to change status stories?
Tap the Status icon on the sidebar rail or navigation bar to post 24-hour disappearing text or photo updates.
